Rider moves

When Front wheels at (%) Delay (s) Weight on front foot (%) Twist (N·m) Twist time (s) Reorder or remove

Moves happen in order, top to bottom, each once the one before it has happened. Add puts a move at the bottom; while a row is selected it adds above or below that row instead.

When: going up and coming down happen once the front wheels are at or past the position; board stops happens once the board stops moving up while it's on the ramp or the coping (not at the top of a jump).

Front wheels at: 0% is the front axle at the start of the curve and 100% is the front wheels touching the coping; below 0% is on the flat before the ramp, and past 100% follows the board over the coping.

Delay: how long after its moment the move happens.

Weight on front foot: moves the rider's body, which is pinned to the board between the feet, towards the front or back foot; it takes the rider a moment to get there (about 0.3 s from one foot to the other). Leave it blank to keep the weight where it is.

Twist: the rider's legs turning the board under their body; positive lifts the nose, negative pushes it down. A short twist of 100 to 200 N·m pops the nose up; longer or harder twists can throw the board into the air. Twists only work while the board is on something; in the air the rider always turns the board to match the surface below, ready to land.

Twist time: how long the twist lasts. Leave it blank for no twist.
